How it works

It changes the source, not just one app

Apps don’t read GPS directly. They ask Android or Google Play services. Falcon Perch answers both, once a second, with your perch.

Google Maps included

Puts Google Play services location into mock mode too, so Wi‑Fi and cell towers can’t give you away.

Travel, don’t teleport

Jump instantly, or walk, cycle or drive to a new perch along a real great-circle route.

Always in control

A quiet notification shows while it’s on, with a Stop button. If Android revokes access, the app tells you.

Saved places

Keep the perches you use, rename them, switch in one tap, and export a backup as JSON.

Privacy log

See every location request and what answered it. Real coordinates are never written down.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no analytics, no ads, no servers. Maps come from OpenStreetMap; searches send only what you type.

Setup

Ready in about two minutes

Android lets one app replace your location, and you choose which one. The app checks each step for you.

  1. 1

    Install the app

    Download falcon-perch.apk on your phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser.

    Download APK
  2. 2

    Unlock Developer options

    Settings → About phone → tap Build number seven times.

  3. 3

    Choose Falcon Perch

    Developer options → Select mock location app → Falcon Perch.

    Select mock location appFalcon Perch
  4. 4

    Flip the switch

    Allow location access when asked, pick your perch and turn on Apply my perch to all apps.

    All apps

Honest limits

What it hides, and what it can’t

Privacy tools should tell you the whole truth. Here it is.

Hidden by Falcon Perch

  • GPS location seen by apps
  • Wi‑Fi and cell-tower location from Google Play services
  • Location in maps, weather, social and shopping apps
  • Location attached to new photos and check-ins

Still visible, so take care

  • Your IP address. Use a trusted VPN with a server near your perch.
  • Your mobile carrier. It always knows which towers you use.
  • Your Google account. Pause Timeline and turn off Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth scanning.
  • Detection. Android marks replaced locations; some banking apps and games may refuse to run.

Emergencies: tap Stop in the notification before calling emergency services, so responders can find you.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to root my phone?

No. Falcon Perch uses Android’s built-in “mock location app” setting, which any phone can turn on from Developer options.

Does it work with Google Maps?

Yes. Google Maps reads location from Google Play services, and Falcon Perch puts Play services into mock mode with your perch. Allow location access when the app asks so it can do this.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. Apple doesn’t let apps change the location other apps see. You can still use the web version to choose the location used inside Falcon Perch itself.

Why isn’t it on the Play Store?

It’s distributed straight from GitHub, where it’s built in the open from public source code. You can check what you install against the code that built it.

Will it drain my battery?

Hardly. It publishes a fix once a second without turning on GPS hardware. Turn it off when you don’t need it.

How do I stop it?

Tap Stop in the notification, or turn off the switch in the app. Apps see your real location again straight away.

Is this legal?

Changing the location your own phone reports is legal in most places, and it’s a standard Android developer feature. Using it to deceive or defraud someone isn’t. Use it to protect your privacy, and follow the laws where you live.

What data does Falcon Perch collect?

None. There’s no account, no analytics and no server. Your perch and saved places live only on your phone, and “Forget everything” wipes them.
